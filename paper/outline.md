# Paper Outline (WP 5.1)

Working title: "The Confounding Frontier: Exact Spectral Bias Laws, Calibrated Detection, and the Limits of Deconfounding under Dense Hidden Confounding"

One-sentence contribution (post-G3 amendment, binding): exact spectral theory for hidden dense confounding: a validated capture law for the bias confounding induces, a calibrated detection test whose power frontier is predictable to within a factor of about one, a proof-backed map of what cannot be detected and by which classes of statistics, and a trim-then-regress recipe for treatment effects, with the failure of tuned soft-trim estimators characterized rather than hidden.

Target tier: AoS / EJS / JRSS-B style (generic article class; theory-plus-diagnosis paper with an honest autopsy section).

## Structure

1. **Introduction** (Sec 1). Problem: dense latent confounding in high-dimensional observational regression; practitioner questions (is confounding biasing me, is it detectable, does spectral adjustment help or hurt). Contributions as numbered list: capture-law theorem (T1) + ridge interpolation + trimmed-tau law (T7); calibrated alarm with predictable frontier (T2-supported); scoped impossibility and visibility boundary (T3a/T3b); hard-trim dominance and the soft-trim kill as characterized negative results (T4/T6); semi-synthetic validation + package. Reading map. Related work: spectral deconfounding family (Cevid et al. 2020 JMLR, Scheidegger et al., Ulmer et al., Nava et al.), confounding strength (Janzing-Schoelkopf 2018; Rendsburg et al. 2024), high-dim endogeneity (Fan-Liao 2014), multiple causes (Wang-Blei 2019), RMT toolkit (MP, BBP, BGN, Johnstone TW, OMH 2013, Onatski 2010, Dobriban-Wager 2018, Hastie et al. 2022).
2. **Model, estimands, assumption ledger** (Sec 2). M1/M2 DGPs; normalizations (spike strengths l_j, c = p/n, theta alignment, Haar loadings Q); estimands incl. the loading-conditional bias functional (why conditional on Lambda: spherical-symmetry argument); identification subtlety (only beta* identified from second moments) and ledger A1-A6 with A4a primary + A4b/c/d variants; detection problem statement (H0 gamma=0 vs H1, statistic family S1/S2/S3, effective spike s_eff); frontier quantities s_detect vs s_remove.
3. **The capture law** (Sec 3). Statement T1: directional mean-bias DE at c>1 via cap_j = (1+l_j)/(c+l_j), exact identity at c<=1; proof route summary (elementary: corrected channel assembly, Haar-probe odd symmetry; no local laws needed); companion identity R2 E[q'Pi q] -> (c-1)/(c+l); fixed-r extension via Woodbury + vanishing lemmas; ridge interpolation with proved cap(lam) = (1+l)m_bar/(1+(1+l)m_bar), lam->0 collapse to min-norm law; trimmed-tau law T7 (FWL + SM identities; shrinkage -tau Lambda_D mechanism; predicted 1.558 vs measured 1.5548 +/- 0.072; trim immunity by [D,S] rank; c-flatness). Numerical validation: correctness overlays (91.7%/100% tiers within 10%; ridge median dev 0.0%), Figure de_overlay_grid.pdf.
4. **Calibrated detection and its frontier** (Sec 4). Statistic S2 maxz_cal construction; finite-n null law (mc95 ~1.7x naive Gaussian-max scale, noise-aware calibration D10 chi2 p=0.41, size band share 0.968 within z2); power frontier predictions vs empirical g80 (median ratio 1.02; strata ratios 0.93/1.02/1.25; all <=1.5), Figure power_surface_vs_frontier.pdf.
5. **What cannot be detected** (Sec 5). T3(a) scoped impossibility: eigenvalue-alarm blindness for sub-BBP dense confounding under response standardization (contiguity, OMH-grounded; pure-X alarms trivially blind by data processing, substantive claim covers augmented-spectrum alarms). T3(b) visibility boundary: quadratic-probe phase curve in (c,g), satiation at |omega*-omega|, MP boundary c=1 where scaling-profile probes are born blind; closed forms verified to <=2%. Le Cam probe honesty: frozen universal-invisibility declaration FAILED and split by c (chance-level at c=0.8 up to g~1.6; AUC up to 1.0 at c=0.2), rescoped claims, Figure lecam_probe_auc.pdf.
6. **What fails for estimation, and what survives** (Sec 6). The kill: soft-trim family reaches no-regret in 4.1% of harmful cells even with oracle tau (need 95%); pca_onatski wins 89/97 cells; sdboost byte-equals OLS in 31 cells (T6 collapse lemma: EB ratio vanishes under A4a so weights -> 1). T4 hard-trim dominance theorem sketch. Trim-then-regress: M2 tau flat across c while OLS inflates ~6x; Figures crossover_curves.pdf / bias_phase_diagram_empirical.pdf.
7. **Semi-synthetic benchmarks and the package** (Sec 7). Families A/B/C (AddNeuroMed GSE63060/61, IHDP-featurized, k401ksubs); six configs massively outlier-positive pre-injection (PF-2 premise); PF-1 substance straddle on all six configs (power 0.14-0.21 at 0.5x g*, 0.98-1.00 at 2x); PF-2 scree fires always incl. permutation nulls; PF-3 tau trim 0.173 vs OLS 0.421 (ratio 2.43, sign 100%, null cost negligible); PF-4 UCM/JS baselines anti-conservative out-of-sample while alarm holds 0.000; G4 mechanical REVIEW -> adjudicated GO transparency note; sensitivity findings (mega-spike concentration reduces detectability; hetero_eps no drift), Figure benchmark_frontier_check.pdf; confounderalarm v0.1.0 description (verdict, p-value, r/l/c estimates, blind-region certificate, Onatski trim recommendation).
8. **Limitations** (Sec 8). A4a scope (linear-Gaussian first; empirical universality V-panels; T5 cut rationale); conservative negative controls (sizes 0.000 below the [0.02,0.10] band from below, mechanistically explained); alignment sensitivity (frontier prediction degrades under mega-spike concentration); computational probe is not an information-theoretic lower bound; r misspecification effects; single M2 economics family.
9. **Discussion** (Sec 9). Sensitivity-analysis orthogonality (Cinelli-Hazlett/E-values quantify hypothetical confounders; we test whether data contain evidence of one and certify when non-detection means nothing); combined workflow; outlook.

## Standing objections to answer (each backed by an artifact)

1. "Why not just PCA-trim?" -> We agree for adjustment: T4 proves when hard-trim dominance holds; but trimming answers nothing about presence/strength/location of confounding; alarm + certificates fill that.
2. "Janzing-Schoelkopf already detect spectrally." -> Head-to-head calibration-vs-informality table: their criterion ships no exact null law or frontier; ours delivers size + predicted power + blind certificates (PF-4 shows their permutation-calibrated variant anti-conservative out-of-sample).
3. "Your probe broke invisibility at c=0.2." -> That is T3(b): boundary derived, not hand-waved; universal claim only for eigenvalue-alarm statistics; quadratic-probe blindness above measured c-boundary.
4. "Sensitivity analysis already handles unmeasured confounding." -> Orthogonality argument; combined workflow demonstrated on benchmarks.
5. "All linear-Gaussian." -> Limitations section states scope explicitly; V-panel universality subsection.
6. "Is the capture law proved or conjectured?" -> Proved at r=1 (exact algebraic identity after corrected bookkeeping), fixed-r reduced and numerically confirmed; adversarial audit verdict PROOF STANDS; falsifiers permanent in tests/test_theory_T1.py.
